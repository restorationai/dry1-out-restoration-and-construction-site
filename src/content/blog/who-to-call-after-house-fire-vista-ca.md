## Who do I call first after a house fire in Vista?

Call 911 first if the fire department isn't already on scene. If crews are there, wait for the officer in charge to confirm the structure is safe before anyone, including you, goes back inside. They check for structural damage, hidden hot spots in walls and attics, and compromised electrical and gas lines.

Don't re-enter the house on your own judgment, even for ten minutes to grab something. Fire crews sometimes stay on scene for hours after visible flames are out, watching for reignition in insulation or wall cavities. Wait for an explicit all-clear from the officer in charge.

## What is the right order of calls after a house fire?

The order is 911, your insurance company, a restoration company, then your utility providers. Each call depends on the one before it, so doing them out of order can cost you time, coverage, or both.

1. **911 / Vista Fire Department.** Gets the fire out and clears the structure for re-entry.
2. **Your homeowners insurance company.** Opens the claim, assigns a claim number, and starts the clock on coverage for things like additional living expenses if the house isn't livable.
3. **A restoration company.** Handles [emergency board-up](/services/emergency-board-up/) of broken windows and compromised doors, [smoke damage restoration](/services/smoke-damage-restoration/), and any water left behind from putting the fire out.
4. **Utilities.** If SDG&E or the water utility shut off service, confirm with them, not just your own eyes, before anything gets turned back on.

## What happens after the fire department clears the house?

Once you have that clearance, the next call is your insurance company, because most policies require prompt notice of a loss. Don't hold off on board-up or mitigation while you wait for a claim number, since acting quickly is part of your duty to mitigate. Call the number on your policy or your agent directly, and have your policy number and the fire department's incident number ready if you have it.

## What should I avoid doing before the restoration crew arrives?

Don't turn the HVAC system back on, since it will pull soot through the ducts and spread it to rooms the fire never touched. Don't wipe soot off walls or fabric with water, since that can set the residue instead of lifting it.

Keep the receipts for anything you buy because of the fire, including a hotel stay or takeout meals, since those can count toward additional living expenses coverage.
