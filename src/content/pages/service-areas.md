Dry1 Out Restoration and Construction is based in Vista and dispatches crews throughout North San Diego County. Because our phones are answered 24/7, a call placed at 2 a.m. gets the same attention as one placed at noon: we log the details, assess the loss, and get a crew moving. Response order depends on loss severity and distance from our Vista base, not on who calls first.

## Where we work

Our team is based in Vista and responds across North San Diego County, from the coastal communities along Highway 78 and I-5 to the inland cities along the I-15 corridor. That range covers a mix of older coastal bungalows with original plumbing, newer inland tract housing, and everything built in between, which means our crews see a wide variety of construction types and water intrusion patterns in a single week.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Vista get the fastest turnaround simply because there's less road between us and the job. As the radius widens toward the edges of North County, arrival takes longer, especially during peak traffic windows on Highway 78 and I-15, but every call still gets dispatched in the order severity demands. Active flooding, sewage backups, and fire damage move to the front of the queue regardless of distance. For jobs at the outer edge of our coverage map, we're upfront on the phone about where your call falls in that queue so you're not left guessing.

## Don't see your city?

If your property isn't listed on our service area pages, call anyway. Coverage maps are built around where we respond most often, not a hard boundary, and we regularly make exceptions for large losses, commercial properties, and multi-unit buildings outside the usual radius. The fastest way to find out if we can reach you is a phone call, not a guess based on a map.
