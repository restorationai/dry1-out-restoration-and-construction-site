Dry 1 Out Restoration and Construction handles the full span of property damage work, from the first call after a pipe bursts through the final coat of paint on the rebuild. We work with homeowners, property managers, and commercial building owners across Vista and the surrounding area, and we're an IICRC Certified Firm, so the same crew that extracts water or scopes a fire loss is trained to the structural drying, mold, and fire and smoke standards that guide the repair.

## Emergency restoration services

Water damage doesn't wait for business hours, and neither do we. We answer 24/7 for water damage restoration, flood damage, burst pipe cleanup, basement flooding, and emergency water removal, along with ceiling water damage repair and water leak detection when the source isn't obvious. Fire damage restoration and smoke damage restoration address both the structure and the soot and odor left behind after the flames are out. Sewage cleanup and sanitization, biohazard cleanup, blood cleanup, crime scene cleanup, and trauma scene cleanup require careful containment and disposal, and we treat each one that way. Storm damage restoration, emergency board up, vandalism cleanup and repair, and vehicle impact damage repair cover the structural breaches that need to be secured fast, before weather or trespassers make the loss worse.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Once a structure is dry, clean, or stabilized, it still has to be put back together. Our reconstruction services pick up directly where mitigation leaves off, rebuilding drywall, flooring, cabinetry, and trim to match what was lost. For projects that go beyond repair, our general contracting team handles renovations and remodels, so a kitchen damaged by a leak or a basement gutted after flooding can come back better than it left, under permits and inspections managed by the same company that handled the loss.

## Specialty services

Not every call starts with standing water. Mold inspection and testing identifies what's actually growing and where, and mold remediation removes it and addresses the moisture source that fed it. Odor removal and deodorization gets rid of smoke, pet, or mildew smells that linger after the visible damage is gone. Carpet water extraction and appliance leak cleanup handle smaller, contained losses before they spread into subfloor or walls. Post-construction and specialty cleaning finishes out renovation and rebuild projects with a thorough clean before a space is handed back. For larger facilities, our commercial and industrial restoration teams scale up for large loss and catastrophic response.

## One call, one company

The handoff between mitigation and reconstruction is usually where a restoration job slows down, waiting on a second contractor to quote, schedule, and start. Dry 1 Out Restoration and Construction keeps that work under one roof, so the crew that dries out a structure is the same company that rebuilds it. If you're not sure which service fits your situation, call (888) 379-1688 and describe what you're looking at. We'll point you to the right page, or the right person.
