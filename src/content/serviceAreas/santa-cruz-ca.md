## Services we provide in Santa Cruz

**Water damage restoration.** Water damage in Santa Cruz often starts as a roof leak or supply line failure that goes unnoticed in a crawlspace until flooring starts to cup. Coastal humidity means standing water doesn't dry on its own the way it might in a drier climate, so moisture mapping and controlled drying matter more here than the square footage of the spill.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration.** Whether the loss is a kitchen fire in a Downtown Santa Cruz apartment or smoke infiltration from a regional wildfire event, soot behaves differently depending on what burned and how porous the surfaces are. We assess odor penetration into insulation and ductwork before deciding what can be cleaned versus what needs to come out.

**Mold remediation.** Coastal fog that sits over neighborhoods like Seabright and the Westside keeps humidity elevated for months at a time, and that's before winter rain adds bulk moisture. Mold doesn't wait for a visible stain. Once a wall cavity or subfloor stays wet past a few days, colonization is already underway, which is why containment and air handling come before demolition, not after.

**Biohazard and trauma cleanup.** These situations call for controlled, documented cleanup following accepted biohazard protocols, handled with discretion for the property and the people affected.

**Reconstruction.** Once drying, demolition, or decontamination is complete, our crews rebuild the affected areas, matching finishes where possible so the repair doesn't read as a patch job.
