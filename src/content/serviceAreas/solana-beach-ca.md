## Restoration emergencies common in Solana Beach

Solana Beach sits directly on the Pacific, and that proximity shapes the kind of damage we see. Salt-laden marine air accelerates corrosion on plumbing fittings, HVAC condensers, and exterior fasteners, which shows up as slow leaks years before a pipe actually bursts. Winter atmospheric river storms off the coast can dump heavy rain in short bursts, and homes built close to bluffs or drainage swales take on water fast when gutters and site grading can't keep pace. Older homes with original cast iron or galvanized supply lines are more prone to pinhole leaks, and coastal humidity gives mold a head start once a slab or crawlspace stays damp. Add in the wildfire smoke that can drift into the area during dry Santa Ana conditions, and property owners here end up calling us for water, mold, and smoke-related work in roughly equal measure.

## Services we provide in Solana Beach

**Water damage restoration.** Coastal leaks in Solana Beach often start small, a corroded fitting, a slow roof intrusion during a winter storm, and go unnoticed until flooring or drywall is already saturated. We extract standing water, pull moisture readings from wall cavities and subfloor, and dry the structure to the IICRC S500 standard rather than guessing by feel.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration.** Whether the loss is a kitchen fire or smoke infiltration from regional wildfire activity, we clean soot from HVAC systems and porous materials, deodorize the structure, and coordinate with your carrier on what's salvageable versus what needs to be rebuilt.

**Mold remediation.** Marine-layer humidity combined with a slab or crawlspace that stayed wet after a leak gives mold the conditions it needs within days. We contain the affected area, remove compromised material, and verify the space is dry before it gets closed back up.

**Storm damage response.** Heavy coastal rain events can push water past roofing, flashing, and site drainage that wasn't built for that volume. We tarp, extract, and stabilize the property so secondary damage doesn't compound the original loss.

**Reconstruction.** Once a space is dry and clean, our construction side handles drywall, flooring, trim, and finish work so you're not managing a second contractor after the mitigation crew leaves.

Much of Solana Beach was built from the 1960s through the 1990s, with a mix of ranch-style, Mediterranean, and mid-century homes on slab-on-grade foundations, which is typical for coastal North County.
