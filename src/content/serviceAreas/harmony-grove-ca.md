## Services we provide in Harmony Grove

Water damage restoration in Harmony Grove usually starts with a slow leak under a slab, a washing machine supply line, or storm water finding its way under a crawlspace vent. We extract standing water, pull moisture readings from framing and subfloor, and dry the structure per the IICRC S500 standard rather than guessing at a timeline.

Fire and smoke damage work here often involves wildfire smoke residue that's settled into stucco, siding, and attic insulation even when the structure itself was never touched by flame. We clean and deodorize surfaces and assess whether HVAC systems pulled smoke particulate through the ductwork.

Mold remediation becomes necessary when a water event sits unaddressed, which happens easily in homes with crawlspaces or in rooms that stay closed off. We contain the affected area, remove compromised material, and verify the space is dry before closing anything back up.

Once the demolition and drying phase is finished, our construction side handles the drywall, flooring, and framing repairs needed to put the property back together, so the same team that mitigated the loss also rebuilds it.
