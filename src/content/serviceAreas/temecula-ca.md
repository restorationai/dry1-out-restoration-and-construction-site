## Restoration emergencies common in Temecula

Temecula's inland valley sits far enough from the coast that summers run hot and dry, which is exactly the setup for Santa Ana wind events and wildfire exposure along the hillside edges of the city. Smoke infiltration into attics and HVAC systems is a real concern even when a fire never reaches a structure directly. On the water side, most damage here starts indoors rather than from rising groundwater: a slab leak under a kitchen in Paloma del Sol, a washing machine supply line failure in a two-story Harveston home, or a water heater past its service life in an Old Town Temecula cottage. When Southern California does get a wet winter, the valley's clay-heavy soils in spots can shed water fast toward foundations instead of absorbing it, and older storm drainage in some pockets of the 92590 area struggles to keep pace with a hard rain.

## Services we provide in Temecula

Water damage restoration is our most frequent call in Temecula, and it usually traces back to a supply line, slab leak, or appliance failure rather than flooding. Biohazard cleanup covers the situations property owners hope they never face, from sewage backups to trauma scenes, and it requires containment and disposal handled correctly the first time.

Reconstruction closes the loop after any of the above: framing, drywall, flooring, and finish work rebuilt to match what was there, coordinated with the same team that handled the mitigation.
