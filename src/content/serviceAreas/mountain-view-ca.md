## Restoration emergencies common in Mountain View

Mountain View sits low and close to the bay, which shapes the kind of damage we see requests for. ## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Mountain View

Much of Mountain View's single-family housing dates to the postwar boom of the 1950s and 60s, built slab-on-grade or over shallow crawlspaces, with newer infill and multifamily construction filling in since. Homes from that era commonly carry galvanized or early copper supply lines and cast iron drain stacks, both of which are well past their expected service life and prone to pinhole leaks and slow drain backups; older interiors may also contain asbestos-containing materials in flooring, texture coat, or pipe insulation that a restoration scope has to account for before demolition. The bay's proximity means a higher water table and heavier clay content in the soil than inland Santa Clara County, which slows drainage around foundations and crawlspaces after storms.
