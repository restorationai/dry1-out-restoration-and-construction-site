**Noticed a dark ring spreading across a ceiling tile, or a musty smell that gets stronger every time the HVAC kicks on?** Visible mold growth after a slow roof leak, a hidden plumbing drip, or last season's flooding doesn't go away on its own, and waiting to find out how far it's spread usually means a bigger remediation bill later. A mold inspection gives you a clear, documented look at what's actually growing and where, so you know what you're dealing with before anyone touches a wall.

## What Mold Inspection actually involves

A mold inspection from Dry 1 Out is a free, visual assessment. One of our IICRC AMRT-trained technicians walks the affected area, looks at the visible growth, checks for moisture staining, bubbling paint, warped drywall, and soft or spongy flooring, and uses a moisture meter to trace how far dampness has traveled behind the surface. We're looking at what's already visible, or confirming what an independent lab has already identified, not collecting samples ourselves. Mold testing should stay separate from the company doing any remediation work, so if your situation calls for air sampling, spore counts, or lab-confirmed species identification, we'll point you to an independent, third-party mold tester. We don't perform that testing, and we don't offer it as part of this service.

Most inspections take under an hour for a typical residential space. Larger properties, crawlspaces, or multi-room water intrusion take longer because the technician needs to trace moisture paths through more wall cavities and flooring sections.

## Our process

1. **Visual walkthrough.** We start where you've seen the growth or discoloration and work outward, checking adjacent rooms, shared wall cavities, and anything directly below or above the affected area.
2. **Moisture mapping.** A moisture meter checks drywall, subfloor, and framing near the visible mold to see how far the dampness has actually traveled, since mold almost always extends past what's visible on the surface.
3. **Source identification.** We trace the moisture back to its cause: a roof leak, a slow appliance drip, condensation on a cold pipe, poor attic ventilation, or grading that pushes water toward the foundation.
4. **Written findings.** You get a clear summary of what we observed, where moisture was detected, and what the likely source is, along with a recommendation on whether remediation is warranted or whether independent testing should happen first.
5. **Next-step referral.** If your situation needs lab-confirmed testing before any cleanup begins, we'll tell you that directly and point you toward an independent tester, not toward ourselves.

## What separates a good mold inspection from a bad one

A rushed inspection looks at the stain on the ceiling and stops there. A thorough one checks the attic space above it, the wall cavity behind it, and the subfloor below it, because mold rarely stays contained to the spot where it first becomes visible. Technicians who skip moisture mapping often miss the fact that a ceiling stain is dry on the surface while the insulation above it is still saturated, which means the growth keeps spreading even after the visible spot gets painted over.

Another common mistake is treating every mold call as a remediation sale. An inspection should give you an honest answer, including when the visible growth is minor enough that it doesn't need a full containment job, or when the real problem is a moisture source that needs fixing before any cleanup will hold. Insurance adjusters reviewing a mold claim generally want documented moisture readings, photos of the affected materials, and a clear link between the mold and a covered water event, like a burst pipe or storm intrusion, rather than long-term humidity or neglect.

## What does a Mold Inspection cost?

The visual mold inspection itself is free when mold is already visible or has been identified by an independent third-party tester. There's no charge to have a technician look at what you're seeing and tell you what they find. If the inspection leads to a remediation recommendation, that work gets its own written scope before anything begins, so you know exactly what's included and what it covers. Homeowners insurance coverage for any resulting mold remediation generally depends on whether the moisture source was a sudden, covered event like a pipe burst versus gradual damage, and we document the findings in a way that supports whichever claim applies.
