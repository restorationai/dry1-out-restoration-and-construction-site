Post-construction cleanup in Berkeley rarely looks like a simple sweep-and-mop. Between plaster dust working into original fir floors in a North Berkeley bungalow and fine debris settling into century-old lath in a Claremont remodel, the final clean has to account for materials a 1920s contractor never imagined would need HEPA vacuuming. ## Why Berkeley Properties See Heavier Post-Construction Cleanup

A large share of Berkeley's housing stock predates 1950, concentrated in neighborhoods like Elmwood and the Berkeley Hills, where brown shingle and Craftsman-style homes still carry original plaster, built-in woodwork, and single-pane windows. Renovation work on these homes kicks up more dust than a typical drywall remodel because plaster and lath generate a finer particulate than gypsum board, and that dust finds its way into heating registers, window tracks, and original hardwood that wasn't designed for fast moisture or chemical cleaning. Add in Berkeley's dry summer winds off the hills and the damp, foggy stretches near the Marina, and dust settles differently depending on which side of town the job sits.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Berkeley

We start with a walkthrough to flag original materials that need gentler handling, old plaster, built-in cabinetry, or reclaimed fir flooring common in Southside and West Berkeley homes near campus. Rough debris and packaging go out first, followed by a full HEPA vacuum pass on floors, sills, and vents to pull fine dust before it gets pushed around with water. We then do detail cleaning on fixtures, cabinetry, and glass, and finish with a final touch pass once the crew confirms no residual dust is resettling from HVAC return air. On occupied properties we schedule around residents and pets, since many of these projects are additions or kitchen remodels where the family is still living in part of the house.

## Berkeley Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most post-construction cleaning jobs we take on are billed directly to the homeowner or coordinated with the general contractor rather than run through a property insurance claim, since this is a finishing service rather than a damage repair. For condo and multi-unit buildings, particularly conversions near Fourth Street and Southside, HOA rules sometimes dictate service elevator hours or require protective floor covering in common hallways during debris haul-out, and we build that into scheduling before the crew arrives.

## Local note

One detail that matters on Berkeley Hills jobs specifically: steep, narrow private driveways and shared access roads mean our debris removal and cleaning equipment sometimes has to stage on the street and get carried in by hand rather than pulled up to the garage. We confirm driveway grade and parking restrictions with the homeowner before the crew shows up so the final clean isn't delayed by a truck that can't make the turn.
