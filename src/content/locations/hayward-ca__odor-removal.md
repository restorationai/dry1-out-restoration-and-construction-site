## Why Hayward Properties See Odor Problems Linger

A lot of Hayward's housing stock, particularly around Downtown Hayward and the older tracts near Tennyson-Alquire, dates to the postwar boom years, with original plaster walls, single-pane windows, and minimal attic ventilation. ## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Hayward

We start by identifying the actual source material, not just the room where the smell is strongest. Smoke and fire odor cling to porous surfaces well beyond the visible burn area, so we inspect attic spaces, HVAC returns, and wall cavities before treating anything. Depending on what we find, we use hydroxyl generators for occupied spaces where people or pets are present (hydroxyl treatment doesn't require evacuation the way ozone does), ozone treatment for vacant units or during a tenant turnover when the space can be sealed off for a cycle, and thermal fogging to drive deodorizing agents into the same pore structure the odor particles penetrated in the first place. For textiles and soft goods that can be saved, we pair this with targeted cleaning rather than just masking the smell with fragrance, since masking doesn't hold up once Hayward's fog rolls back in overnight.
