## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Valley Center

We start by walking the property to separate wet smoke residue (thick, oily, from slow-burning materials) from dry smoke residue (fine, powdery, typical of fast wildfire-driven ash), because the cleaning agents and pressure settings differ for each. HEPA vacuuming comes before any wet cleaning step, since wiping dry soot too early just grinds it into fabric and grout. Hard surfaces get cleaned with technician-grade degreasers matched to the residue type, while HVAC systems get inspected and, when soot has reached the coils or ductwork, cleaned or isolated so odor doesn't keep recirculating through the house. For lingering odor in framing lumber or subflooring, we use thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment rather than masking sprays, because masking agents fade and the underlying odor returns within weeks. Content cleaning for upholstery, linens, and personal items is handled separately so the restoration schedule for structural areas isn't held up waiting on soft goods.
