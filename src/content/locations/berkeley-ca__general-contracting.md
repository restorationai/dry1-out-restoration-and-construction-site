A kitchen remodel or post-damage rebuild in one of these properties isn't a cookie-cutter job: the framing, the wiring, and even the window openings often don't match what a contractor expects from a standard 2x4 stud layout. Dry 1 Out Restoration and Construction handles renovations, remodels, and general contracting work across Berkeley with that older-building reality built into how we plan and price every project.

## Why Berkeley Properties Need a Different Remodeling Approach

A large share of homes in North Berkeley and Southside predate 1950, which means plaster-and-lath walls, original fir flooring, and knob-and-tube wiring remnants are still common finds once a wall opens up. Permit requirements through the City of Berkeley's Permit Service Center also tend to scrutinize structural changes more closely in these older homes, especially near the Alquist-Priolo earthquake fault zones that run through parts of the hills. A bathroom remodel that looks straightforward on paper can turn into a conversation about seismic retrofitting, knob-and-tube replacement, or lead-safe work practices the moment demolition starts, which is why scoping a project here means budgeting for discovery, not just finish selections.

## Our Renovations, Remodels and General Contracting Process in Berkeley

We start with an in-person walkthrough that includes moisture and structural checks, not just a finish-selection conversation, because so many Berkeley properties carry some history of water intrusion from older roofing or grading that no longer directs runoff away from the foundation. From there we draft a scope that separates code-driven work (electrical panel upgrades, seismic bracing) from the cosmetic remodel the homeowner actually called about. For kitchen and bathroom remodels specifically, we sequence plumbing and electrical rough-in before finish work so inspections happen at the right stage and the project doesn't stall waiting on a city sign-off. When a remodel follows water or fire damage, our IICRC-trained team handles the structural drying and smoke remediation first, then transitions directly into rebuild without handing the job to a second contractor.

## Berkeley Permitting and HOA Coordination

Single-family remodels in Berkeley typically go through the standard city permit process, but properties in condo developments or shared-driveway lots, which show up more often in West Berkeley and parts of Claremont, usually require HOA sign-off on exterior changes before the city will finalize anything. We pull permits under our general contractor license (CA #993442) and handle the paperwork sequencing so homeowners aren't stuck waiting on two separate approval tracks at once. For post-damage rebuilds tied to an insurance claim, we document the original damage and scope separately from the remodel upgrades the homeowner is adding, since carriers will only cover the former and that distinction matters when the adjuster reviews the estimate.

## Local note

Homes built before 1960 in the flatter blocks near Fourth Street and the Southside district often sit on shallow perimeter foundations that were never engineered for today's seismic code. When we open walls for a remodel in these areas, we flag any foundation anchoring gaps to the homeowner early, because addressing it during an open-wall phase is far less disruptive than retrofitting it later as a standalone project. It's a detail that doesn't show up on a typical scope sheet until someone is already inside the wall cavity.
