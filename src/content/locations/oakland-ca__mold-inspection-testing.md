## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Oakland

An inspection starts with a visual assessment of the areas where occupants have noticed odor, staining, or past water intrusion, followed by moisture meter readings on wall cavities, subfloor framing, and any area with a history of leaks. Our AMRT-credentialed technicians use thermal imaging to spot temperature differentials that point to trapped moisture behind finished surfaces without opening walls unnecessarily. Where conditions warrant it, we collect air samples with calibrated spore trap cassettes, comparing indoor readings against an outdoor baseline, and take surface swabs on any visible growth. Samples go to an independent accredited lab, and results typically come back within a few business days with a written report outlining whether remediation is warranted and what scope it would involve.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold Inspection

Beyond moisture meters and thermal cameras, we use borescopes to look inside wall cavities through small access points, which saves homeowners from unnecessary drywall cuts during the inspection phase. Hygrometers log ambient humidity over the visit so readings aren't skewed by a single dry or foggy day, which matters in a climate where conditions shift block to block depending on proximity to the bay. All lab analysis is handled by a third-party facility, not performed in-house, so results stay independent of any recommendation we make about next steps.

## Oakland Insurance & HOA Coordination

Condo and townhome associations near Jack London Square and other multi-unit developments frequently require documented mold inspection results before approving interior work, and some require a third-party clearance report before a unit can be re-occupied after remediation. We provide the documentation format HOAs typically ask for, including lab chain-of-custody records. On the insurance side, most policies treat mold from a sudden, covered water event differently than mold from long-term, unaddressed moisture, so our report notes the apparent source and duration where it can be determined, which carriers use to evaluate coverage.
