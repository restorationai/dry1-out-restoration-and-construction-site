## Our Trauma Scene Cleanup Process

Every job begins with a scene assessment to identify all affected materials, porous and non-porous, and to determine what can be cleaned versus what needs to be removed. Our crews, trained under IICRC protocols, use EPA-registered disinfectants and controlled containment to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. Porous materials like carpet padding, subfloor, or drywall that cannot be fully decontaminated are removed and disposed of according to biohazard waste regulations. Once cleaning is complete, we run air quality and surface checks before releasing the space back to the family or property manager, and we document every step for insurance and for peace of mind.
