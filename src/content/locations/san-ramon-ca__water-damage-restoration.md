## Why San Ramon Properties See Water Damage Issues

Many San Ramon homes sit on hillside or sloped lots, which is great for views but means drainage and grading do a lot of the work keeping water out during heavy rain. When gutters overflow or a yard's grading has settled over a few decades, water finds its way toward foundations and crawlspaces rather than away from them. Combine that with aging water heaters, washing machine supply lines, and dishwasher connections in homes now several decades old, and appliance and plumbing failures are just as frequent a cause of water damage calls as storm intrusion.
