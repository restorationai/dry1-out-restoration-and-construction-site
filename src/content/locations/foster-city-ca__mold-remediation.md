When drywall, insulation, or cabinetry starts showing the telltale gray-green bloom or that flat, musty smell, the fix depends on understanding what's pushing moisture into the structure in the first place, not just what's growing on the surface.

## Why Foster City Properties See Mold Issues

Foster City sits on reclaimed bayland, and most of its housing stock dates to the 1960s and 1970s slab-on-grade construction built directly over that fill. Slab foundations this close to the bay sit above a consistently high water table, and vapor drive through concrete is a normal condition here, not an anomaly. Add the marine layer humidity that rolls in off the bay most mornings, plus lagoon-adjacent landscaping that keeps soil saturated near foundations, and you get interior humidity levels that stay elevated longer than in drier East Bay cities. Bathrooms, laundry closets, and any room backing onto a lagoon-facing wall are where we'd expect moisture to accumulate first, and mold colonization can start within 24 to 48 hours once a surface stays damp.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Foster City

We start with containment, sealing off the affected area with poly sheeting and negative air pressure so spores don't migrate into HVAC returns or adjoining rooms, which matters in the condo and townhome clusters common throughout the city where units share walls and sometimes ductwork. From there we remove and bag contaminated material following HEPA-filtered air scrubbing, dry the substrate per IICRC S520 guidelines, and apply antimicrobial treatment before any rebuild begins. On slab-built homes we also check subfloor moisture content near exterior walls and lagoon-facing rooms, since surface drying can look complete while the slab underneath is still releasing vapor. Our team holds IICRC AMRT mold certification, and every step is documented with photos and moisture readings for your insurance file.

## Local note

Because so much of Foster City sits on bay fill rather than natural soil, vapor barriers under older slabs have often degraded or were never installed to current standards, and we regularly find moisture wicking up through concrete in homes where the walls and carpet test dry but the slab itself reads elevated. Skipping a subfloor moisture check in these homes is how mold comes back within a season of a surface-only cleanup.
