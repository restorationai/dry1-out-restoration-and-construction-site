## Our Vandalism Damage Cleanup and Repair Process in San Jose

We start by documenting the damage thoroughly, photos and measurements you can hand to your insurance adjuster or attach to a police report, since both are usually required before a claim moves forward. From there:

- **Test the surface.** A small, inconspicuous patch tells us whether the stucco, brick, or siding can handle solvent-based graffiti remover or needs a gentler approach.
- **Remove and neutralize.** Spray paint, etching, and marker get lifted without grinding away the texture coat underneath, which is the difference between a repair and a patch job that stands out in direct sunlight.
- **Board and secure.** Broken windows, pried doors, or damaged entry points get boarded the same visit so the property isn't sitting exposed while repairs are scheduled.
- **Repair and repaint.** Drywall, siding, and trim get patched and color-matched. In HOA communities around Rose Garden and Evergreen, that often means pulling the original paint code rather than eyeballing a match.
