## Why Santa Cruz Homes See Hidden Moisture and Mold

A lot of Santa Cruz's housing stock predates modern vapor barrier requirements, especially in neighborhoods like Seabright and Downtown Santa Cruz where single-wall and post-and-pier construction is still common. Combine that with the coastal fog, winter storms rolling off Monterey Bay, and the high water table near the San Lorenzo River corridor, and you get crawlspaces that stay damp for days after a storm even when the roof and gutters are doing their job. Bathrooms without adequate ventilation, older single-pane windows that condense heavily overnight, and under-sink cabinets near beach-adjacent properties near Natural Bridges State Beach are the spots we're asked to look at most often.

## Our Free Visual Mold Inspection Process

When mold is already visible, or a homeowner has had independent lab testing done and wants someone to walk the property and assess the extent of it, we schedule a free visual inspection. An IICRC-trained technician walks the affected area, checks adjoining materials like drywall, baseboards, and subfloor for moisture intrusion with a meter, and photographs what's visible so you have documentation for your own records or for an insurance adjuster. We're not there to collect air samples or swab a wall for a lab, we're there to look at what's in front of us and tell you plainly what we see and what it would take to remediate it.

## Keeping Testing Independent

If mold hasn't been confirmed yet, or you want spore counts or clearance testing done before or after remediation, that work has to come from an independent mold tester, not from us. We keep inspection, remediation, and testing separated on purpose, so the person assessing the problem isn't the same company billing for the cleanup. If you've already had a third-party test done in Santa Cruz, bring us the report and we'll use it to scope the remediation; if you haven't, we can point you toward testing before we do anything further.
