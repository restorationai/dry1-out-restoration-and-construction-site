## Our Mold Inspection Process in San Diego

When mold is already visible, or a homeowner has had a sample tested by an independent lab, our team comes out to do a visual inspection of the affected area and surrounding materials. We look at the extent of surface growth, check for moisture intrusion points with a moisture meter, and document what we find with photos for your records or an insurance claim. We don't collect air samples or swab tests ourselves. We treat mold testing and mold remediation as separate functions for good reason, so if sampling or lab analysis is needed, we point you to an independent mold tester who isn't also bidding the cleanup. That keeps the results neutral and keeps the remediation scope honest.
