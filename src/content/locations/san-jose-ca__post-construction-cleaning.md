## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process

We start with a walkthrough to identify what kind of debris the job left behind, fine drywall dust, paint residue, adhesive overspray, or construction grit tracked into flooring seams. Air scrubbing and HEPA vacuuming come before any wet cleaning so we're not just pushing dust from the air into damp surfaces. Cabinets, window tracks, and light fixtures get detailed by hand since construction dust settles into hardware and hinges that a general cleaning crew tends to skip. Flooring gets matched to material, hardwood and tile in Willow Glen's older homes need different treatment than the engineered flooring common in newer Almaden Valley builds. We finish with a final HVAC vent and filter check, since a system left running during framing or drywall work often needs its filters swapped before the homeowner moves back in.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Post-Construction Cleaning

HEPA-filtered air scrubbers handle the airborne dust that standard shop vacuums recirculate back into the room. We use microfiber systems on cabinetry and trim rather than feather dusters, which just relocate fine particles. For paint overspray on glass or tile, we use scraper tools rated for the surface rather than solvents that can haze finished stone or leave residue on new countertops. On jobs with adjacent structural or reconstruction work, we coordinate the final clean timing so it happens after punch-list items are closed, not before, which keeps the house from needing a second pass.
