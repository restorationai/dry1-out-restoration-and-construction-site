Our mold inspection is a free visual walkthrough, not a lab test, and it's built around spotting the kind of mold growth San Jose's housing stock and weather actually produce.

## Why San Jose Properties See Mold Issues

Santa Clara Valley gets long dry summers followed by a concentrated wet season, and that swing is hard on both old and new construction. Older homes in Willow Glen, Rose Garden, and Japantown were often built with raised foundations and crawlspace vents that were never meant to handle decades of landscaping changes, additions, and patched stucco. When those vents get blocked or foundation grading shifts, humid air gets trapped under the subfloor and condenses against cool framing. On the other end, newer tract construction in Evergreen and parts of Berryessa is built tighter for energy efficiency, which is good for utility bills but means a single leaking appliance line or an undersized bathroom fan can push indoor humidity up fast with nowhere for it to go. Hillside lots in Almaden Valley and Cambrian Park add another variable: homes cut into a slope often have retaining walls and grade changes that direct water toward the foundation instead of away from it.

## Our Free Mold Inspection Process in San Jose

We start outside, looking at grading, downspout placement, and crawlspace vents, since drainage problems usually show up indoors months later as a stain or a smell. Inside, we check the usual trouble spots: under sinks, around tub and shower surrounds, behind washing machines, in attic sheathing near roof penetrations, and along baseboards in rooms that share a wall with a bathroom. We use moisture meters to read framing and drywall moisture content and document anything visible with photos. If mold is already visible, that's enough to move forward with a scope of work. If it isn't clear whether what you're looking at is mold, mineral staining, or something else, we'll say so plainly rather than guess.

## Keeping Inspection and Testing Independent

We don't collect air samples, swab samples, or send anything to a lab, and we won't quote you for that work. California property owners are generally better served when the company inspecting or remediating mold isn't also the one testing for it, so if your situation calls for sampling, we'll point you to an independent mold testing company to handle that step. If you've already had a third-party test done, bring us those results and we'll build our visual inspection and any remediation scope around what the lab found. This keeps the diagnosis and the cleanup separate, which matters if you're also working with an insurance adjuster or an HOA board that wants documentation before approving work. In condo and townhome associations around Almaden Valley and parts of Cambrian Park, boards sometimes want to see an independent report before authorizing common-area or shared-wall work, and having that paperwork ready speeds up approval.

## Local note

In Willow Glen and the Rose Garden, a lot of the 1920s to 1940s bungalows still have their original foundation vents, and we routinely find them partially buried under mulch beds or hardscaping added during a landscaping project years after the house was built. That blocked airflow traps humid crawlspace air against the floor joists year-round, and it's one of the most common sources of a musty smell in homes that otherwise look well maintained. It's a quick thing to check and an easy one to miss if you're not looking for it specifically.
