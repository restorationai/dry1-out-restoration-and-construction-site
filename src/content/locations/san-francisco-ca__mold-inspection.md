## Why San Francisco Properties See Mold Issues

San Francisco's building stock works against itself when it comes to moisture. A lot of the housing in the Richmond and Sunset Districts went up mid-century on sand-based lots close to the ocean, with crawlspaces that stay damp most of the year from fog drip and poor cross-ventilation. Pair that with older single-pane windows that condense heavily in winter, and you get a slow, steady moisture load that drywall and old plaster can't shed fast enough. In denser pockets like SoMa and the Mission, converted warehouses and tightly packed Victorians often have shared walls and minimal airflow between units, so a leak in one unit can show up as a mold patch two doors down before anyone traces the source. None of this means every stain is a crisis, but it does mean San Francisco homes tend to hide moisture problems behind finished surfaces longer than homes in drier inland cities.

## Our Free Mold Inspection Process in San Francisco

We start with a walkthrough of the affected area, looking at staining patterns, texture, and odor, and we check adjacent spaces, attic access, and crawlspaces where accessible. Our IICRC-trained team documents what's visible with photos and notes on moisture readings at the surface, which helps establish whether the growth is active or old. What we don't do is collect air samples or swabs ourselves. If the extent or type of mold needs lab confirmation, we point you to an independent mold tester so the person assessing the problem is never the same company proposing the cleanup. That separation keeps the process honest, and once you have results (or if the mold is already visibly confirmed), we can scope remediation based on the inspection findings.

## San Francisco Insurance & HOA Coordination

A good share of San Francisco's housing is condo or co-op stock, especially in Pacific Heights, SoMa, and parts of Noe Valley, and many of those buildings have HOA rules requiring documentation before any remediation work begins in shared walls or common areas. We photograph and log everything from the inspection so you have a clear record to hand to your HOA board or property manager, and the same documentation supports an insurance claim if the mold traces back to a covered water loss, like a slow pipe leak behind a kitchen wall near Golden Gate Park's eastern edge.

## Local note

Homes within a few blocks of the water in the Sunset and Richmond, roughly the 94121 and 94122 ZIPs, often show mold along exterior-facing walls even when there's no obvious leak, because the stucco and older sheathing absorb fog moisture directly through hairline cracks. We check those exterior walls as a matter of course on inspections in those neighborhoods, since the problem is as much about the coastal climate as it is about any single plumbing issue.
