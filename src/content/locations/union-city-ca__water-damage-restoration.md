## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Union City

The first thing we do on any water loss is figure out what kind of water it is and where it's actually traveled, not just where it's visible. On slab homes, that often means moisture mapping under flooring and along baseboards with a meter rather than assuming the damage stops at the waterline. We extract standing water, pull up or cut out saturated flooring and drywall where it won't dry in place, and set drying equipment calibrated to the room's size and the materials involved. Structural drying on an older Union City home with original lath or dense subfloor can take longer than it would in newer construction, because those materials release moisture more slowly even after the surface reads dry.
