## Why Del Mar Properties See Mold Issues

Del Mar sits right against the Pacific, and that proximity cuts both ways. The marine layer that rolls in most mornings keeps outdoor air cool and damp for hours before it burns off, and that humidity works its way into crawlspaces, attics, and north-facing walls that rarely see direct sun. Homes closer to the bluffs and lower-lying areas near the lagoon tend to have higher ambient moisture year-round, which is exactly the kind of steady dampness mold needs to establish itself, usually within 24 to 48 hours of a hidden leak or chronic condensation issue. Add in the area's mix of older beach cottages with original subfloor venting and newer coastal builds with tighter building envelopes, and you get two very different mold patterns depending on when a house was built. Older homes tend to show it in crawlspaces and baseboards; newer, tightly sealed homes tend to trap it behind walls where ventilation is weaker than it looks on paper.

## Our Mold Inspection Process in Del Mar

This is a free visual inspection, not a lab test. Our technician walks the affected areas, looks for visible growth, moisture staining, and conditions that support mold (bad flashing, a slow plumbing leak, poor crawlspace ventilation), and gives you a clear picture of what's there and where it's likely coming from. What we don't do is collect air samples, swab samples, or send anything to a lab. California property owners are often surprised to learn it's standard practice to keep mold testing and mold remediation separate, so if your situation calls for air sampling or spore counts, we'll point you to an independent, third-party mold tester rather than run that testing ourselves. That keeps the inspection and any later remediation work honest and independently verified, which matters most when you're dealing with an HOA, an insurance carrier, or a future buyer's inspection report.
