Because much of the area sits close to the water table maintained by the lake itself, crawlspaces and lower-level rooms near the shoreline tend to hold humidity longer after any intrusion, which matters for how long we need to run equipment before a space reads dry.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Lake San Marcos

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted or portable units depending on how far a unit sits from street access. From there we map the loss with moisture meters and, where needed, thermal imaging to find water that's migrated into wall cavities or under flooring rather than staying where it fell. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard: air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the room and material, not a flat setup repeated at every job. In the slab-foundation homes common here, we pay close attention to flooring adhesives and underlayment, since trapped moisture under tile or vinyl plank can sit for a long time before it shows up as cupping, odor, or a soft spot underfoot. We monitor daily readings until materials hit dry standard, then document everything for the file.
