Dry 1 Out Restoration and Construction handles the structural side of that moment: stabilizing the opening, documenting the damage for your claim, and rebuilding the wall, framing, or foundation ledge the vehicle hit.

## Why Hayward Sees This Kind of Damage

Hayward's street grid mixes steep hillside blocks in the Highlands with flat postwar neighborhoods like Fairway Park and Mt. Eden, where many homes were built in the 1950s and 60s with attached garages set close to the curb. Driveways that slope sharply toward a garage door, common throughout the Highlands, give a car in neutral or with a parking brake failure more momentum before it meets a wall. Downtown Hayward's older commercial corridors, particularly the storefronts along B Street and Mission, sit with minimal setback from the road, which means a vehicle leaving the roadway has very little buffer before it reaches a building face. Tennyson-Alquire's mix of single-family homes and small multi-unit buildings also tends to have garages and carports built directly into the front elevation, which puts framing and siding at direct risk from a low-speed collision.

## Our Vehicle Impact Damage Repair Process

We start by securing the site, since a vehicle-damaged wall or garage opening is rarely just cosmetic. If framing members are compromised, we shore the structure before anyone, including the homeowner, goes back inside that section. From there our team photographs and measures the full extent of the damage, not just the obvious hole, because impact force often travels through a stud bay or header further than it looks from the street. We board up any exposed opening to protect the interior from weather and entry, then move into framing repair, stucco or siding matching, and drywall or masonry rebuild. Where the impact affected a garage slab, retaining wall, or foundation ledge, we bring in the structural assessment before closing the wall back up, since Hayward's hillside soils in the Highlands can shift slightly after an impact disturbs a retaining structure. Reconstruction runs through our general contracting team so the repair is permitted and inspected the same way any other structural work would be, not patched over.

## Hayward Insurance Coordination

Most vehicle impact claims in Hayward come through the driver's auto policy, the property owner's homeowner policy, or both, depending on who's at fault and what the policy limits look like. We document the damage with the kind of photo and measurement record an adjuster expects: point of impact, depth of structural penetration, and a clear before-and-after scope of the rebuild. For homes in Hayward Highlands developments governed by an HOA, exterior repairs, especially stucco color matching or fence and wall rebuilds visible from the street, sometimes need HOA architectural approval before work closes out. We've found it faster to flag that early with the property owner rather than finish a repair and then have to revisit the finish coat for an HOA sign-off.

## Local note

One thing we watch for on Hayward jobs: homes near Cal State East Bay and the Highlands above it were often built on fill and graded slopes, and a vehicle impact to a retaining wall or garage foundation in that area can mask a slope stability issue that has nothing to do with the collision itself.
