## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Temecula

Every job starts with containment: we isolate the affected area to stop cross-contamination before any cleanup begins. Technicians work in full PPE following OSHA bloodborne pathogen guidelines, removing and disposing of contaminated materials as regulated medical waste through licensed handlers. Hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected with EPA-registered hospital-grade products, and porous materials like carpet padding, subflooring, or drywall are typically removed rather than treated, since biological contamination can penetrate deeper than it appears. Once the area is clean, we run air scrubbers and, where needed, ozone or hydroxyl treatment to address lingering odor. We document every step with photos for your insurance file and close out the job with a final inspection before anyone re-enters the space.
