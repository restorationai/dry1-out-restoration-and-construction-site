## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Chula Vista

We start with a visual walkthrough and moisture mapping using a penetrating moisture meter on suspect walls, baseboards, and subfloor access points. Where we can't see directly, a borescope lets us look inside wall cavities without cutting large openings, which matters in finished Eastlake homes where homeowners don't want unnecessary drywall damage.

Air sampling comes next: we set spore trap cartridges in the suspect area and a control sample outside or in an unaffected room, since spore counts only mean something in comparison. If a surface shows visible growth, we take a tape lift or swab sample directly. Everything goes to a third-party accredited lab, and results typically take a few business days. We don't interpret the lab report as a verdict on health risk; we hand you clear numbers and photos so you, your insurer, or a remediation contractor can make an informed decision on next steps.
