## Why South San Francisco Properties See Fire Damage Complications

South San Francisco's housing stock runs older in many pockets, post-war bungalows and duplexes with knob-and-tube or early romex wiring that wasn't built for the electrical load of modern households. Add in the city's dense mix of residential blocks sitting close to light-industrial and biotech corridors, and a kitchen or garage fire often means soot traveling further through shared walls and attic spaces than it would in newer construction. The Bay's marine layer compounds the problem after suppression: damp, cool air keeps smoke odor trapped in porous materials like insulation and subfloor longer than it would in a drier inland city, which is why odor work here can't be rushed.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in South San Francisco

We start with a walkthrough to separate what's salvageable from what isn't, then move into water extraction where fire suppression left standing water in flooring and wall cavities. Soot is categorized by type (protein, synthetic, or dry smoke) because each reacts differently to cleaning agents, and we document everything with photos before any demolition begins. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized for the humidity South San Francisco carries most of the year, not a dry-climate setup that would leave moisture behind the walls. Odor removal is its own phase: thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment where porous materials can be saved, controlled demolition where they can't.
