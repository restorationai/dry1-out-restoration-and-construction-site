## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in San Diego

We start with a walk-through to classify the fire: protein, synthetic, or wildfire-driven smoke each leave a different residue signature, and that classification decides which cleaning agents we use. HEPA vacuuming pulls loose soot off surfaces before we touch anything with a cleaning solution, because scrubbing dry soot into porous material like unsealed plaster or raw wood trim just drives it in deeper. From there we move to wipe-downs calibrated to the surface, thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment for odor that's worked into HVAC ducting or wall cavities, and a final air quality pass before we call the job closed. In older homes with plaster or horsehair lath, that odor-neutralizing step often takes longer than the actual cleaning, since porous plaster releases trapped smoke slower than it absorbed it.
