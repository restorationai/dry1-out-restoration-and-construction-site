## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Mountain View

Every job starts with containment: we isolate the affected area to protect the rest of the property and anyone inside it. From there, our technicians remove and properly dispose of biohazard material following state and local regulations for medical and biological waste, then clean and disinfect all affected surfaces. Porous materials like carpet padding, drywall, or untreated wood often can't be fully sanitized and are removed rather than treated. Once cleaning is complete, we verify the space meets our standard for return-to-use before we close out the job. We work quietly and discreetly, understanding that families, tenants, or neighbors nearby don't need details, they need the situation resolved.
