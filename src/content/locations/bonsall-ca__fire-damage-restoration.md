## Why Bonsall Properties See Fire Damage Issues

Bonsall sits in the kind of wildland-urban interface that North San Diego County is known for: dry brush, grove rows, and canyon topography that channel Santa Ana winds through the valley in late summer and fall. A lot of the housing stock out here is older ranch construction with wood siding, open eaves, and long gravel or paved driveways separating the home from the road. Those features look charming until an ember lands on a wood shake roof or a cedar fence line and the fire has a running start before anyone notices. Properties with wells and septic systems also face an added wrinkle: a fire that knocks out electrical service can take the well pump down with it, which affects everything from water for cleanup to basic habitability while repairs are underway.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Bonsall

Work starts with a walkthrough to separate structural damage from surface soot and smoke residue, since those two problems get treated very differently. Charred framing, scorched stucco, and compromised electrical panels get assessed for structural fire damage before anyone starts cleaning, because reconstruction decisions have to come first. From there the crew works through soot removal on exposed wood and stucco exterior (common on Bonsall's ranch and hacienda-style homes), content cleaning for smoke-saturated furniture and fabrics, and odor treatment using HEPA air scrubbing and thermal fogging where smoke has worked into open-beam ceilings or hardwood flooring. Drying and deodorizing follow IICRC S500 and fire-specific protocols, and the team carries FSRT (Fire & Smoke) certification alongside its general IICRC firm certification.
