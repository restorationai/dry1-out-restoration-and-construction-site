## Keeping Inspection and Testing Independent

We don't collect air samples, swab samples, or send anything to a lab, and we don't price out testing services. If a property needs spore counts or clearance testing, whether for a real estate transaction, a landlord dispute, or just peace of mind, that work needs to come from an independent mold testing company with no stake in the remediation.
