## Why Poway Properties See Mold Issues

Poway sits further from the coastal marine layer than Del Mar, Encinitas, or Solana Beach, so homes here deal with wider temperature swings between day and night. That swing drives condensation on cooler surfaces like attic ductwork, cold water lines, and north-facing exterior walls, especially in homes with older or undersized HVAC systems. Add in drip and spray irrigation common on larger Poway lots, often set close to stucco exteriors or stem walls, and you get a steady moisture source that can wick into wall cavities for months before anyone notices a stain. Slab foundations, common across many Poway tracts built from the 1960s through the 1980s, can also develop small leaks at plumbing penetrations that show up first as a soft, cool spot in flooring rather than standing water.

## Our Mold Inspection Process in Poway

Our visual mold inspection is free, and it stays exactly that: a visual inspection. We walk the affected area, check surrounding rooms and attic or crawlspace access where relevant, and use a moisture meter to map how far dampness has traveled behind drywall or under flooring. If we see mold growth, we document it with photos and explain what we're seeing and why. We do not collect air samples, swabs, or tape lifts, and we don't run lab testing ourselves. If a property owner needs confirmation beyond what's visible, we point them to an independent mold testing lab or industrial hygienist, so the inspection and any later remediation work stay handled by separate parties. That separation isn't a formality to us; it's how we keep our recommendations trustworthy, since we're never grading our own test results.
