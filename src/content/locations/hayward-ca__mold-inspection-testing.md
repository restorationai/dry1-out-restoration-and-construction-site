## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Hayward

We start with a visual assessment of the areas you're concerned about, then move to moisture readings on walls, subfloors, and around plumbing penetrations using a moisture meter and, where needed, a thermal camera to spot hidden wet zones behind finished surfaces. If conditions warrant lab confirmation, we collect air samples and, when there's visible growth, surface tape or swab samples, which are sent to an independent lab for species identification and spore count comparison against outdoor baseline air. This baseline comparison matters in a coastal climate like Hayward's, where outdoor spore counts shift seasonally and a one-off indoor number means little without context. You get a written report with lab results, moisture findings, and a plain-language explanation of whether remediation is warranted and where.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold Inspection

Beyond moisture meters and thermal imaging, we use particle counters and pump-based air sampling equipment calibrated to collect a consistent air volume, which keeps results comparable from room to room and from your indoor baseline to the outdoor control sample. For crawlspaces, a common trouble spot in Hayward's older homes near Cal State East Bay and the flatter shoreline-adjacent tracts, we use a borescope to inspect inside wall cavities and under subfloor insulation without unnecessary demolition. Our inspectors hold IICRC AMRT mold training, which shapes how we interpret sampling results and where we recommend testing versus where a moisture reading alone tells the story.
