## Why Camp Pendleton South Properties Need Renovation-Ready General Contracting

Most homes and housing units in and around Camp Pendleton South were built to serve military families on compressed timelines, which means plumbing runs, electrical panels, and finish materials are often standardized rather than custom. That's efficient for original construction, but it creates headaches during a remodel: non-standard fixture spacing, shared utility chases between adjoining units, and finish materials that are no longer stocked by the original manufacturer. Add in the coastal marine layer that rolls through this stretch of San Diego County most mornings, and you get accelerated corrosion on exterior fasteners, window frames, and HVAC line sets that inland contractors don't plan for. A kitchen or bathroom remodel here needs materials rated for that salt-air exposure, not just whatever's cheapest at the supply yard.
