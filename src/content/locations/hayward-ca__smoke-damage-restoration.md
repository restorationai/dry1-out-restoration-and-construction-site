## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Hayward

We start with an inspection to classify the smoke, wet smoke from a slow-burning structure fire behaves differently than the dry, fine particulate from a wildfire event, because the cleaning chemistry and dwell time are not the same. From there the process generally runs: contents triage and pack-out where needed, dry sponge or chemical sponge cleaning on porous surfaces like plaster and wood trim, HEPA vacuuming to lift embedded particulate without grinding it deeper into the material, and thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment to neutralize odor at the molecular level rather than masking it. Sealing and priming come last, once odor testing confirms the treatment held. Skipping that step is the most common reason smoke smell comes back weeks later in a home that looked clean right after the work.
