## Why Dublin Properties See Mold Issues

Tri-Valley summers run hot and dry, which sounds like the opposite of a mold-friendly climate, but the swing matters more than the average. Attics and wall cavities in Dublin's stucco-clad tract homes can trap humidity from irrigation overspray, bathroom exhaust fans that vent into the attic instead of outside, or AC condensate lines that drip slowly into insulation for months. Add in the clay-heavy soils common across the East Bay, which hold moisture against slab foundations and stem walls longer than sandier soil would, and you get the kind of slow, hidden dampness that shows up as a musty smell or a faint gray patch on drywall long before anyone sees standing water.

## What a Free Mold Inspection in Dublin Covers

When you call about visible mold, mold inspection near me, or a stain you're not sure about, a technician comes out to look at what's actually there: the size and pattern of the growth, the material it's on, and whether there's an active moisture source feeding it.
