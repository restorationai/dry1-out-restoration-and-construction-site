## What Our Free Mold Inspection Covers

When you call about visible mold, our inspection is a visual assessment, not a lab test. We don't run the testing ourselves. ## Our Mold Inspection Process

The inspection itself is straightforward. We start at the point you've noticed a problem, whether that's a ceiling stain, a musty smell near a water heater closet, or visible growth in a garage near Carlsbad Premium Outlets.
