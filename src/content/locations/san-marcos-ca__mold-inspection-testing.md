A lot of San Marcos housing stock falls into two eras with two different risk profiles. ## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in San Marcos

We start with a visual and moisture-meter survey of the areas most likely to hold hidden moisture: subfloor framing, attic sheathing, and the wall cavities below any plumbing penetration. From there we pull air samples and, where visible growth is present, surface samples, and send both to an independent lab for species identification and spore-count comparison against outdoor baseline air. That baseline step matters more in San Marcos than in a lot of inland cities, because the marine layer changes the outdoor spore count through the day, so timing the outdoor sample correctly keeps the comparison honest. Results typically take a few business days, and we walk the homeowner through what the counts mean before recommending any remediation scope.
