## Why Sunnyvale's Eichler-Era Homes Complicate Renovation Scopes

A large share of Sunnyvale's housing stock, particularly around Heritage District and Ponderosa Park, sits on concrete slab foundations with copper radiant heating tubing embedded directly in the concrete.
