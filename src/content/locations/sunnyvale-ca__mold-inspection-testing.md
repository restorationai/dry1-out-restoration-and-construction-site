## Why Sunnyvale Properties See Mold Growth

Mid-century tract homes across the Heritage District, Cherry Chase, and Lakewood Village were built with minimal attic ventilation and, in many Eichler and Eichler-adjacent models, no attic space at all. Flat roofs pond water after a winter storm instead of shedding it, and if the built-up roofing has aged past its service life, that water finds its way into ceiling insulation or wall cavities slowly, over weeks, rather than all at once. Add in the marine-layer humidity that rolls off the Bay most mornings, and you get interior surfaces that stay damp just long enough for spore colonies to establish, often behind furniture or in closets with poor air circulation. Newer two-story construction closer to Lakewood Village has fewer slab-related issues but still deals with condensation on single-pane or early dual-pane windows common to the era, which is a frequent source of sill and frame staining we get called out to evaluate.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Sunnyvale

We start with a visual walkthrough and moisture mapping using a penetrating meter, paying particular attention to slab edges, baseboards, and any area near radiant heat zones if the home has them. From there we pull air samples in the suspect room and a control sample from outside or an unaffected area of the house, because spore counts only mean something in comparison. If we find visible growth or a moisture reading that doesn't match the surface appearance, we add surface tape lifts or swabs so the lab can identify genus and confirm whether what's growing is consistent with a chronic leak or a one-time event. Samples go to a third-party accredited lab, and results typically take a few business days, after which we walk you through what the numbers actually mean for your specific property rather than handing over a report full of lab jargon.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold Inspection

Thermal imaging helps us spot temperature differentials behind drywall or under flooring without cutting into finished surfaces first, which matters in homes where owners want to avoid unnecessary demolition. We pair that with hygrometers to track relative humidity room by room, since a reading above roughly 60% sustained over time is often enough to support colonization even without a visible source. Spore trap air sampling and, where appropriate, swab or bulk material sampling round out the assessment so the lab report reflects what's actually in the air you're breathing, not just what's visible to the eye.
