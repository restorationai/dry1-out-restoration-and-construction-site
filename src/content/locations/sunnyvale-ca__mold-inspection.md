## Why Sunnyvale's Eichler-Era Homes Show Mold Patterns

A large share of Sunnyvale's neighborhoods, including pockets of Heritage District and Lakewood Village, were built during the mid-century Eichler boom, with post-and-beam construction, radiant slab heating, and flat or low-pitch roofs. Those roofs drain slowly and are prone to ponding during the rare but intense winter storms that move through the South Bay, and slab foundations with aging radiant tubing can develop hidden moisture that works its way up through flooring. Add in the marine-layer humidity that settles over Sunnyvale on summer mornings before burning off by midday, and bathrooms or laundry rooms with weak exhaust fans stay damp longer than they should. None of that guarantees mold, but it explains why we see it show up in the same spots across the same handful of home styles.

## Our Free Mold Inspection Process in Sunnyvale

Our mold inspection is a visual assessment, not a lab test. One of our IICRC-trained technicians walks the affected area, documents what's visible with photos, checks moisture readings on surrounding materials, and looks for a likely source, a roof leak, a plumbing drip, poor ventilation. If mold is already visible, or if an independent third-party mold tester has already confirmed it, we can scope a remediation plan from there. What we don't do is collect air samples, swab surfaces, or send anything to a lab ourselves. California property owners are generally better served keeping testing and remediation separate, so if you need spore counts or a formal clearance report, we'll point you to an independent mold tester and pick up the remediation work once that's done. That separation protects you, and it's not something we cut corners on.
